If you're looking for happy hour in North Park, Louisiana Purchase runs its bar program Monday through Friday from 3 PM to 5 PM — craft cocktails and a short list of Creole bar bites, no reservation required. Here's what's actually worth ordering, and how to plan around it.

## When Happy Hour Runs

Happy hour is a weekday-only window: **3 PM to 5 PM, Monday through Friday**. It doesn't run on Saturday or Sunday, since the kitchen moves straight into [weekend brunch](/brunch) service instead. If you're coming specifically for the happy hour pricing, a weekday visit is the move — see the full [Happy Hour menu](/happy-hour) for current offerings.

## What to Order

The bar program at Louisiana Purchase is built around the same New Orleans-rooted cocktail list served the rest of the day — happy hour doesn't mean a separate, scaled-down menu. A few worth knowing before you sit down:

- **Hurricane** — house rum, fassionola, and a Pop Rocks rim. The most theatrical pour on the list.
- **Prailine Old Fashioned** — bourbon, praline, and Creole bitters, for anyone who wants something slower and more classic.
- **French Margarita** — Martel cognac, curaçao, lemon, lime, and agave, a house twist on a familiar format.

The full [cocktail menu](/menu/cocktails) runs well beyond these three, and the bar is happy to walk you through what's currently pouring.

## Where to Sit

Louisiana Purchase's bar seating is first-come, first-served, which makes it a genuinely easy weekday stop — no reservation, no waiting for a table. If the plan is to stay for dinner afterward, that's the point where booking ahead starts to matter, particularly heading into a Friday night. You can see the full [dining room and patio](/the-space) if you're deciding where to post up.

## Getting There

Louisiana Purchase sits on University Ave at the corner of 23rd Street in North Park, San Diego — right in the middle of the University Ave corridor, so it's an easy stop whether you're starting or ending your evening in the neighborhood. Directions and contact details are on the [contact page](/contact).

## The Bottom Line

If North Park happy hour is on your list, Monday through Friday from 3 to 5 is the window, the bar is walk-in only, and the cocktail program is worth building an evening around — not just a discounted add-on before dinner somewhere else.
