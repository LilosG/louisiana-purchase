Planning a private event in North Park comes down to one question: how much will it actually cost? Louisiana Purchase offers a full-service venue with Creole-inspired cuisine, a wraparound patio, and an indoor dining room—but pricing depends on your guest count, menu selections, bar package, and timing. This guide breaks down exactly what you'll pay and how to maximize value for your event.

## Understanding Louisiana Purchase Private Event Pricing Structure

Private event costs at Louisiana Purchase fall into three main categories: venue rental, catering, and beverages. Unlike some venues that bundle everything into one price, Louisiana Purchase separates these so you control your spend at each level.

**Venue Rental Fee**: The base rental covers access to the private dining room, wraparound patio, and full kitchen facilities. This fee typically ranges from $1,500 to $3,500 depending on your event day and guest count. Weekday events (Monday through Thursday) are generally 20–30% less expensive than Friday and Saturday bookings. Peak season (May through October and November through December holidays) commands higher rates than January through April.

The venue fee includes tables, chairs, basic linens, and use of the patio space. If your event requires additional setup—like a ceremony space, dance floor, or special décor—discuss those needs early with the events team, as they may involve extra fees or require external vendor coordination.

**Catering Per-Person Cost**: Food pricing at Louisiana Purchase starts at $35–$55 per person for standard Creole menu packages and can reach $75–$95+ per person for premium options or custom dishes. These packages typically include an appetizer course, entrée selection, and dessert. The kitchen excels at dishes like crawfish étouffée, blackened fish, gumbo, and jambalaya—signature items that define the restaurant's identity and justify the per-person investment.

For a 75-person event with a mid-range menu at $50 per person, catering alone runs $3,750. Scale that to 150 guests, and you're looking at $7,500 just for food. Smaller events (30–50 guests) may have a 10–15% per-person surcharge to cover kitchen labor and setup costs.

## Beverage Package Options and Their Impact on Total Cost

Beverages are where private event costs can fluctuate most dramatically. Louisiana Purchase offers three primary bar structures:

**Full Open Bar**: Premium and well spirits, wine, beer, and house cocktails. Pricing typically runs $15–$20 per person for a 3–4 hour event. For a 100-person event, that's $1,500–$2,000 in bar costs alone. This option works best for corporate events, milestone celebrations, and guests who expect robust cocktail service.

**Premium Beer and Wine with Select Spirits**: A middle-ground option at $8–$12 per person. You get quality wine selections, craft beers, and 2–3 signature cocktails but skip premium liquor. This saves roughly 40% compared to a full bar and appeals to events where not all guests are heavy drinkers.

**Signature Cocktail Package**: The most cost-effective choice at $5–$8 per person. The kitchen creates 2–3 house cocktails (think Sazeracs, Hurricane-style drinks, or Creole-inspired originals) plus beer and wine. This strategy works well for receptions, rehearsal dinners, and events with a specific vibe you want to emphasize.

**Cash Bar or Limited Service**: Some hosts opt to cover only wine and beer ($3–$5 per person) and let guests purchase premium drinks. This lowers your liability but can feel less generous to guests.

For a realistic 100-person event with a mid-tier open bar, expect $1,500–$2,000 in beverage costs. That's roughly 20–25% of your total event budget.

## Maximizing Value: Where to Invest and Where to Save

**Invest in the Menu**: The Creole-inspired cuisine is Louisiana Purchase's core strength. Paying $55–$65 per person for quality proteins and house-made sauces delivers tangible value. Guests remember food more than décor. Cheaper menus ($35–$40 per person) often rely on filler sides and less distinctive proteins.

**The Wraparound Patio is Included**: This is a major value driver. Most North Park venues charge extra for outdoor space or don't have it at all. Louisiana Purchase's patio extends your usable square footage, allows for cocktail receptions, and creates visual interest. You're not paying extra for this—it's part of your venue rental.

**Negotiate Bar Timing on Weekdays**: If your event is Tuesday or Wednesday, ask about discounted bar packages for 2–3 hours instead of 4. You can save $300–$500 this way without guests feeling shortchanged.

**Consider a Signature Cocktail Strategy**: Instead of a full open bar, work with the kitchen to develop 2–3 house cocktails tied to your event theme. This feels intentional, controls costs, and guests often find it memorable. **Timing Matters More Than Season**: A Thursday in October costs significantly less than a Saturday in October. If your date is flexible, shifting one day can save $500–$1,000 on venue rental alone.

## Hidden Costs and What's Included

**Gratuity**: Most venues add 18–20% gratuity to food and beverage totals. At Louisiana Purchase, this is typically automatic for parties over 20 guests. Budget an extra $1,500–$2,000 on a $7,000–$8,000 event.

**Service Charges**: Some events incur a 3–5% service charge on top of food and beverage. Clarify this upfront.

**Setup and Breakdown**: Louisiana Purchase typically handles this as part of the venue fee. No surprise charges for table setup or patio arrangement.

**Outside Vendors**: If you bring in a photographer, DJ, or florist, confirm they're allowed and whether there's a vendor fee (usually $100–$300 per vendor). Louisiana Purchase is generally vendor-friendly.

**Dietary Accommodations**: Gluten-free, vegan, and allergy-specific meals rarely cost extra if communicated in advance. Last-minute requests may incur surcharges.

**Alcohol Overage**: If your event runs longer than quoted, additional bar service is charged at an hourly rate (typically $150–$250 per hour). Keep this in mind when setting your timeline.
