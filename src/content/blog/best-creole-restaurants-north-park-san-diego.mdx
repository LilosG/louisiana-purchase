North Park has no shortage of restaurants, but genuine Creole cooking is rare here. Louisiana Purchase brings New Orleans technique to San Diego, built around a menu that takes gumbo, jambalaya, and Gulf seafood seriously.

## What Makes It Real Creole Cooking

A proper roux takes time. Louisiana Purchase's kitchen builds its gumbo the traditional way, layering flavor over hours rather than rushing it. That commitment carries through the rest of the menu, from blackened catfish to red beans and rice.

## The North Park Setting

The dining room and bar bring a New Orleans energy to a North Park block, with a cocktail program built around classic and modern Creole drinks. Whether it's a weeknight dinner or a weekend crowd, the space handles both comfortably.

## Visit Louisiana Purchase

Louisiana Purchase is open for dinner and weekend brunch in North Park. Reservations are recommended for larger groups.
